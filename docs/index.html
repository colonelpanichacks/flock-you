How to Flash

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop/laptop (Web Serial is not available on mobile or Safari/Firefox).
  2. Select your hardware from the dropdown above.
  3. Connect your device via USB.
  4. Click Install and select the correct serial port from the browser dialog.
  5. Wait ~30 seconds for the flash to complete — the device will reboot automatically.
  6. Open a serial monitor at 115200 baud to confirm it's scanning.

What It Detects

Flock You passively monitors 802.11 probe requests and BLE advertisements for signals associated with Flock Safety infrastructure.

  • 35 Flock Safety Wi-Fi OUIs (field-verified MAC prefixes)
  • 6 contract-manufacturer OUIs (Liteon / USI)
  • SoundThinking / ShotSpotter infrastructure (d4:11:d6)
  • BLE device names: FS Ext Battery, Penguin, Flock, Pigvision, Raven
  • BLE manufacturer ID 0x09C8 (XUNTONG / Flock Safety)
  • Raven camera GATT service UUIDs (8 UUIDs)
